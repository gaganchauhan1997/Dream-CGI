Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools free?

Yes. The local tools work without a paid API key or account.

Do my prompts leave my browser?

These local tools do not send your prompt text to a server. Avoid entering confidential information on any website you do not trust.

Does DreamCGI provide real YouTube analytics?

Not currently. The dashboard charts above are explicitly illustrative. Private analytics would require authorized account connections.

Does the planner generate actual AI videos?

No. It generates structured text plans for use in compatible third-party video tools.
